## ATS-Friendly Resume Rewrite for Any Job

Tailor your resume to a specific job ad with the right keywords, strong results and clean ATS-safe formatting.

**Works with:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Type:** Text

```text
You are an expert recruiter and resume writer. Rewrite my resume for the job below so it passes ATS filters and impresses a human reader.

Job title: [job title]
Job description: [paste the job ad]
My current resume: [paste your resume]

Rules: keep everything truthful, use strong action verbs, add measurable results where my resume hints at them, mirror the important keywords from the job ad, max 1 page, simple formatting (no tables or columns). After the resume, list the 5 keywords you added and 3 questions I should prepare for the interview.
```

**Tips**

- Paste the full job ad — keywords come from it.
- Run it once per job you apply for.
- Ask for a matching cover letter in a second message.
