## Hand-Painted Anime Scene from Your Photo

Redraw any photo as a soft, nostalgic hand-painted anime still with watercolour skies and warm sunlight.

**Works with:** ChatGPT Images, Nano Banana, Midjourney, Leonardo AI  
**Type:** Photo

```text
Redraw the uploaded photo as a hand-painted anime film still. Keep the same people, poses, clothing colours and composition. Soft watercolour backgrounds, gentle pastel colours, warm afternoon sunlight, fluffy clouds, delicate line art, expressive eyes, cozy nostalgic mood, detailed nature (grass, flowers, trees). No text, 16:9.
```

**Tips**

- Photos outdoors or with pets give the most charming results.
- Add "rainy evening, glowing street lamps" for a moody version.
- Describe the style instead of naming a studio — it gives more stable results.

Example results and the interactive prompt builder: [Hand-Painted Anime Scene from Your Photo](https://freeaiprompts.net/prompt/hand-painted-anime-scene-from-your-photo)
