## Explain Any Topic Like I’m 10 (3 Levels + Quiz)

Understand anything fast: a simple analogy, then high-school and university explanations, plus a quick quiz.

**Works with:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, DeepSeek  
**Type:** Text

```text
Explain [topic] to me like I'm 10 years old. Use one everyday analogy, short sentences and no jargon. Then explain it again at a high-school level, and finally like I'm a university student, adding the correct technical terms. End with a 5-question quiz (with answers hidden at the bottom) so I can check that I really understood it.
```

**Tips**

- Great for homework, exams and tricky work topics.
- Ask for a diagram description or a mnemonic to remember it.
- Use Perplexity if you need sources to cite.
