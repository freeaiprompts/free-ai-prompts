## Minimal Modern Logo Design

Create clean, flat, professional logo ideas with your brand name in seconds — great with Ideogram and Midjourney.

**Works with:** Ideogram, Midjourney, ChatGPT Images, Leonardo AI  
**Type:** Photo

```text
Design a modern minimal logo for "[Brand Name]", a [type of business, e.g. specialty coffee shop]. Flat vector style, simple geometric icon that represents [idea: e.g. a coffee bean and a sunrise], clean sans-serif wordmark, [2] brand colours: [deep green and cream]. Centered on a white background, no gradients, no mockup, high contrast, scalable, suitable for an app icon and signage.
```

**Tips**

- Ideogram writes brand names most accurately.
- Generate 8–12 options, then refine your favourite.
- Ask for "monochrome version" to check it works in one colour.

## Your Bestselling Novel Book Cover

Star in your own bestseller! A professional novel cover with you as the hero, your title and your name as the author.

**Works with:** ChatGPT Images, Ideogram, Midjourney  
**Type:** Photo

```text
Design a bestselling novel cover starring the person from the uploaded photo as the main character. Keep the face, skin tone, eyes and hairstyle exactly the same as in the uploaded photo. Genre: [mystery thriller]. The person stands in [a foggy old town street at night] looking over the shoulder, dramatic cinematic lighting. Big elegant title "[THE LAST LETTER]" and the author name "[YOUR NAME]" at the bottom, a small "#1 Bestseller" badge. Show the hardcover book standing on a bookstore shelf, photorealistic, 2:3 vertical.
```

**Tips**

- Change the genre: romance, fantasy, sci-fi, cookbook.
- Use ChatGPT Images or Ideogram for clean title text.
- Great for authors to test cover ideas.
- Make a series for a fun book-club post.

## Glowing Neon Sign Portrait

Your face in glowing neon! A line-art neon sign portrait on a brick wall with your own word or name — perfect for profile pics.

**Works with:** ChatGPT Images, Midjourney, Ideogram  
**Type:** Photo

```text
Turn the person from the uploaded photo into a glowing neon sign portrait. Keep the person recognisable: same face shape, eyes, hairstyle, hair colour and key features as in the uploaded photo, drawn with continuous bent glass neon tubes in [hot pink and electric blue], simple elegant line-art style, with the word "[DREAM BIG]" in neon handwriting below. The sign hangs on a dark red brick wall in a moody bar, neon glow reflecting on the bricks, slight haze, night photography, photorealistic, 4:5 vertical.
```

**Tips**

- Side-profile photos make beautiful neon line art.
- Change the word to your name or brand.
- Try "warm white neon" for an elegant look.
- Businesses: great for café or salon signage ideas.
