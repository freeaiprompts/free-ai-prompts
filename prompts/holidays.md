## Thanksgiving Dinner Table Food Photo

A magazine-style overhead photo of a festive Thanksgiving feast — perfect for menus, blogs and social posts.

**Works with:** Midjourney, ChatGPT Images, Flux, Leonardo AI  
**Type:** Photo

```text
Overhead food photography of a festive Thanksgiving dinner table for [8] people: golden roast turkey in the centre, mashed potatoes, cranberry sauce, roasted pumpkin, green beans, apple pie, autumn leaves and candles on a rustic wooden table, linen napkins, [warm amber] evening light, rich colours, editorial magazine style, ultra detailed, 3:2.
```

**Tips**

- Swap turkey for a vegetarian centrepiece if you like.
- Use it for restaurant menus, blog headers or social posts.
- In Midjourney try --ar 3:2 --style raw for a natural look.

## Thanksgiving Autumn Family Portrait

The perfect Thanksgiving family photo: pumpkins, golden autumn light and matching cozy sweaters — without booking a photographer.

**Works with:** Nano Banana, ChatGPT Images, Gemini  
**Type:** Photo

```text
Create a warm Thanksgiving family portrait from the uploaded photo. Keep the face, skin tone, eyes and hairstyle exactly the same as in the uploaded photo for every person. [The family] sits together on a porch decorated with pumpkins, hay bales, autumn wreaths and warm string lights, wearing [cozy autumn sweaters in rust, mustard and cream], hugging and laughing. Golden-hour sunlight through orange and red autumn trees, falling leaves, professional family photography, natural poses, photorealistic, 4:5 vertical.
```

**Tips**

- Upload one photo where everyone's face is visible.
- Add the dog: "and our golden retriever".
- Use as a Thanksgiving card or invitation.
- Make a horizontal 3:2 version for Facebook.
