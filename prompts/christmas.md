## Christmas Family Photo by the Fireplace

Turn any family photo into a cozy Christmas portrait with matching sweaters, a glowing tree and a crackling fireplace — faces stay the same.

**Works with:** Nano Banana, ChatGPT Images, Gemini, Midjourney  
**Type:** Photo

```text
Transform the uploaded photo into a warm Christmas family portrait. Keep every person's face, hairstyle and identity exactly the same. Place [the family] on a cozy sofa in front of a crackling fireplace with stockings, a tall decorated Christmas tree with warm fairy lights and wrapped gifts. Everyone wears matching [red knitted Christmas sweaters]. Soft golden light, shallow depth of field, shot on an 85mm lens, photorealistic, magazine quality, 4:5.
```

**Tips**

- Upload one clear, well-lit photo where all faces are visible.
- Say "keep faces exactly the same" — it is the most important line.
- Change the sweater colour to match your family style.
- For pets, add "and our dog sitting in front".

## Personal Letter from Santa for Kids

Create a magical, personalised letter from Santa with your child’s name, age and good deeds — ready to print in seconds.

**Works with:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Type:** Text

```text
Write a magical letter from Santa Claus to [child's name], who is [age] years old and lives in [city]. Mention that Santa saw how they [a good thing they did this year] and that the elves loved their wish for [gift]. Add one gentle encouragement for next year about [something to improve]. Make it warm, funny and full of North Pole details (reindeer names, the workshop, cookies). About 200 words, simple words a child can read, signed "Love, Santa".
```

**Tips**

- Fill in real details — that is what makes kids believe.
- Print it on parchment-style paper for extra magic.
- Ask for "a version in rhymes" for younger children.

## Christmas Card Messages for Anyone

Five heartfelt, funny or professional Christmas card messages for family, friends, your boss or clients — plus a gift-tag version.

**Works with:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek  
**Type:** Text

```text
Write 5 different Christmas card messages for [recipient: e.g. my boss / grandma / best friend / clients]. Tone: [warm / funny / professional]. Each message 2–4 sentences, mention [one personal detail], avoid clichés like "season's greetings". Then give one short version (max 12 words) that fits on a gift tag.
```

**Tips**

- Generate separate sets for family, friends and work.
- Ask for a version without religious wording for clients.
- Add an inside joke as the personal detail.
