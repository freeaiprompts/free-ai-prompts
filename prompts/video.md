## Winter Wonderland Cinematic Drone Video

A dreamy 8-second drone flight over a snowy Christmas village — ready for Sora, Veo, Kling and Runway.

**Works with:** Sora, Veo, Kling, Runway, Grok Imagine  
**Type:** Video

```text
Cinematic 8-second video: a slow drone shot gliding over a tiny snow-covered alpine village at blue hour, warm lights glowing in wooden chalet windows, smoke rising from chimneys, a lit Christmas tree in the town square, gentle snowfall, the camera slowly pushes in towards [a cozy cabin with a red door]. Soft orchestral mood, shallow fog in the valley, 24fps, film grain, photorealistic, 16:9.
```

**Tips**

- Keep one camera move per clip — AI video handles it best.
- For vertical Reels/TikTok change 16:9 to 9:16.
- Generate 2–3 clips and join them in CapCut for a full intro.
