## 3D Action Figure of Yourself in a Toy Box

The viral trend: turn your photo into a collectible action figure in blister packaging with your own accessories and name.

**Works with:** ChatGPT Images, Nano Banana, Gemini  
**Type:** Photo

```text
Create a realistic 3D action figure of the person in the uploaded photo, same face, inside a clear blister toy package. The figure stands on the left; on the right side of the package are accessories in their own slots: [a laptop, a coffee cup, headphones, a camera]. The cardboard backing is [teal and orange] with the name "[YOUR NAME]" in bold toy-brand letters and the subtitle "[Content Creator Edition]". Glossy plastic reflections, studio product lighting, collectible toy photography.
```

**Tips**

- Choose 3–4 accessories that describe your job or hobby.
- Short names look best on the package.
- Ask for "Barbie-style pink box" or "retro 80s box" for variations.

## Vintage 90s Polaroid Photo Effect

Give any photo the real 90s Polaroid look: flash glare, faded colours, film grain and a handwritten caption.

**Works with:** Nano Banana, ChatGPT Images, Flux  
**Type:** Photo

```text
Make the uploaded photo look like a real vintage 1990s Polaroid instant photo. Keep the people exactly the same. Slight flash glare, soft focus, faded warm colours, light film grain, a tiny light leak in the corner, white Polaroid frame with a handwritten caption "[summer '98]" in blue marker. Place the Polaroid on a [wooden table] with a few other photos scattered around.
```

**Tips**

- Works great with group photos with friends.
- Change the caption to a date or inside joke.
- Ask for "taped to a fridge" for a different scene.

Example results and the interactive prompt builder: [Vintage 90s Polaroid Photo Effect](https://freeaiprompts.net/prompt/vintage-90s-polaroid-photo-effect)

## Giant Hand Holding a Tiny Version of You

Meet mini-you! A surreal but realistic photo of you holding a tiny version of yourself in your palm — guaranteed double-takes.

**Works with:** Nano Banana, ChatGPT Images, Gemini  
**Type:** Photo

```text
Create a surreal, realistic photo: the person from the uploaded photo holds a tiny, fully detailed miniature version of themselves (about 10 cm tall) in the palm of their hand. Keep the face, skin tone, eyes and hairstyle exactly the same as in the uploaded photo for both the big and the tiny person. The tiny figure [waves happily and sits on the thumb], wearing the same outfit. The big person looks at it with a surprised smile. Natural daylight in [a cozy café], shallow depth of field, focus on the hand and the miniature, photorealistic, 4:5 vertical.
```

**Tips**

- A waist-up photo with visible hands works best.
- Make the tiny you do something funny, like drinking from your coffee cup.
- Try "tiny me sleeping in my shirt pocket".
- Great for reels with a zoom-in.

## Holographic Trading Card of Yourself

Become a rare collectible! A shiny holographic trading card of you with your own title and funny stats about your life or job.

**Works with:** ChatGPT Images, Nano Banana, Ideogram  
**Type:** Photo

```text
Create a holographic collectible trading card featuring the person from the uploaded photo. Keep the face, skin tone, eyes and hairstyle exactly the same as in the uploaded photo. Dynamic heroic pose, the card has a shiny rainbow holographic foil border, the name "[YOUR NAME]" at the top, the type "[Coffee-Powered Designer]" and three stat bars below: [Creativity 98, Focus 72, Snacks 100]. Rare-card gold star in the corner. The card is held in a hand above a desk, light reflecting on the foil, macro photography, photorealistic, 4:5.
```

**Tips**

- Use your job or hobby as the card type.
- Make cards for your whole team — great for office fun.
- Funny stats get the most shares.
- Keep text short so it renders cleanly.

## You on a Retro Cereal Box Cover

Become the mascot of your own retro breakfast cereal — bubbly logo, milk splash and a "free toy inside" badge. Pure 90s fun.

**Works with:** ChatGPT Images, Ideogram, Nano Banana  
**Type:** Photo

```text
Put the person from the uploaded photo on the front of a colourful retro 1990s breakfast cereal box as the mascot. Keep the face, skin tone, eyes and hairstyle exactly the same as in the uploaded photo, with a big cheesy smile and a thumbs up, wearing [a bright tracksuit]. The cereal is called "[CRUNCHY YOU]" in bold bubbly letters, with a bowl of colourful cereal and milk splash in front, a "free toy inside" badge and cartoon stars. The box stands on a kitchen counter in morning light, product photography, photorealistic, 4:5.
```

**Tips**

- Use your name in the cereal title, e.g. "Crunchy Nina".
- Great as a funny birthday gift idea.
- Ask for "healthy granola" for a modern version.
- For brands: put your product mascot on the box.

## Your Pet as the Office CEO

Who's the boss? Your dog or cat as a very serious CEO in a glass office — the funniest pet photo you'll post this year.

**Works with:** Nano Banana, ChatGPT Images, Gemini  
**Type:** Photo

```text
Turn the pet from the uploaded photo into the serious CEO of a big company. Keep the exact breed, fur colour and markings. The pet sits upright in a large leather office chair behind a huge desk, wearing [a tiny navy suit and red tie], with [reading glasses] on the nose, a nameplate saying "CEO", a laptop, coffee and stacks of papers. Glass office with a city skyline behind, professional corporate portrait lighting, very serious expression, photorealistic, 4:5 vertical.
```

**Tips**

- Pets looking straight at the camera work best.
- Add the pet's name to the nameplate.
- Try other jobs: chef, pilot, judge, astronaut.
- Make a "meet the team" post with all your pets.
