## Viral Instagram Caption Generator

Get 10 scroll-stopping Instagram captions with hooks, calls to action and the right hashtags for your niche.

**Works with:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Meta AI  
**Type:** Text

```text
Write 10 Instagram captions for a post about [describe your photo or product]. My account is about [niche] and my audience is [audience]. Mix styles: 3 short and punchy, 3 storytelling, 2 funny, 2 with a question to boost comments. Each caption gets a strong first line (hook) and a clear call to action. Then suggest 15 relevant hashtags (5 big, 5 medium, 5 niche) and the best time to post.
```

**Tips**

- The more specific your niche, the better the hooks.
- Ask for emoji-free versions for a premium brand.
- Reuse the best caption for TikTok and Facebook.

## 60-Second YouTube Shorts & TikTok Script

A ready-to-film short video script with a strong hook, on-screen text, B-roll notes and title ideas.

**Works with:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity  
**Type:** Text

```text
Write a 60-second YouTube Shorts / TikTok script about [topic] for [target audience]. Structure: 0–3s a shocking or curious hook, 3–45s three quick valuable points with examples, 45–55s a surprising twist or tip, 55–60s a call to follow. Write the spoken text in a natural, energetic voice, and add [ON SCREEN TEXT] and [B-ROLL] notes for each part. Also give 3 title ideas and a thumbnail text of max 4 words.
```

**Tips**

- Test the hook — if it doesn't make you curious, ask for 5 more.
- Read it out loud and time it.
- Ask for a long-form 8-minute version from the same idea.

## You as the TV Weather Presenter

Breaking news: you're the weather presenter! A realistic TV broadcast screenshot with your name in the banner and a snowy forecast.

**Works with:** Nano Banana, ChatGPT Images, Gemini  
**Type:** Photo

```text
Turn the person from the uploaded photo into a TV weather presenter live on the evening news. Keep the face, skin tone, eyes and hairstyle exactly the same as in the uploaded photo. [The person] stands in a modern TV studio pointing at a big weather map of [your country] full of snow and sun icons, wearing [a smart blue suit], with a lower-third banner on screen: "[YOUR NAME] – Weather". Studio lights, camera framing like a real broadcast screenshot, slight screen glow, photorealistic, 16:9.
```

**Tips**

- Change the forecast to a joke, e.g. "100% chance of pizza".
- Make the map your own city.
- Perfect for April Fools or birthday posts.
- Try "sports reporter" or "news anchor" too.
