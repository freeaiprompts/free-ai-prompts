## Professional LinkedIn Headshot from a Selfie

Get a clean, studio-quality business headshot from a simple selfie — without changing how you look.

**Works with:** Nano Banana, ChatGPT Images, Leonardo AI, Adobe Firefly  
**Type:** Photo

```text
Turn this selfie into a professional LinkedIn headshot. Keep my face, skin tone, hairstyle and features exactly the same — do not beautify or change identity. Head and shoulders, slight smile, looking at the camera, wearing a [navy blazer with a white shirt], soft studio lighting, clean [light grey] background with subtle gradient, sharp focus, natural skin texture, corporate photography, square 1:1.
```

**Tips**

- Use a selfie taken in daylight, facing a window.
- Remove glasses glare by saying "no reflections on glasses".
- Make 3 versions with different blazer colours and pick the best.

## Cozy Winter Cabin Coffee Portrait

Slow winter vibes: a calm cabin portrait with coffee, a fireplace and snowy mountains outside your window.

**Works with:** Nano Banana, ChatGPT Images, Leonardo AI  
**Type:** Photo

```text
Create a cozy winter lifestyle portrait from the uploaded photo. Keep the face, skin tone, eyes and hairstyle exactly the same as in the uploaded photo. [The person] sits by a large window in a wooden mountain cabin, wrapped in [a chunky knitted blanket], holding a ceramic coffee mug, with snowy pine forest and mountains outside. A crackling fireplace and a stack of books nearby, warm soft light, calm content expression, film photography look, natural colours, photorealistic, 4:5 vertical.
```

**Tips**

- Relaxed, looking-away photos fit best.
- Add "a sleeping dog by the fireplace".
- Change to "Scandinavian minimalist cabin" for a modern look.
- Great for Pinterest winter-aesthetic boards.

## Ice Skating Rink Candid Winter Portrait

A joyful, candid ice-skating photo under Christmas lights — looks like a real photographer caught the moment.

**Works with:** Nano Banana, ChatGPT Images, Flux  
**Type:** Photo

```text
Turn the uploaded photo into a candid winter photo at an outdoor ice skating rink. Keep the face, skin tone, eyes and hairstyle exactly the same as in the uploaded photo. [The person] glides on the ice laughing, wearing [a white puffer jacket, a pastel knitted beanie and mittens], cheeks slightly pink from the cold. ## See Yourself at 80 Years Old
```

What will you look like at 80? A realistic, warm aging portrait that keeps you recognisable — emotional and fascinating.

**Works with:** Nano Banana, ChatGPT Images, Gemini  
**Type:** Photo

```text
Show the person from the uploaded photo realistically aged to [80 years old]. Keep the same identity, face shape, eye colour and features so they are clearly recognisable, adding natural wrinkles, grey hair, age spots and kind laugh lines. The older person sits in [a sunny garden with roses], wearing a cozy cardigan, smiling warmly, holding a cup of tea. Soft natural light, honest documentary portrait style, photorealistic, 4:5 vertical.
```

**Tips**

- Use a front-facing photo with neutral lighting.
- Try 60 or 100 years for a series.
- Make a couple version: "growing old together".
- Post it side by side with your current photo.
