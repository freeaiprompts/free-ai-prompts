## Product Photo for Your Online Store

Turn a phone snapshot of your product into a high-end studio shot for Shopify, Etsy, Amazon or ads.

**Works with:** ChatGPT Images, Nano Banana, Flux, Adobe Firefly, Midjourney  
**Type:** Photo

```text
Professional e-commerce product photo of [product: e.g. a glass bottle of organic face serum] based on the uploaded photo — keep the exact product shape, label and logo. Place it on a [beige stone podium] with [dried flowers and soft shadows], clean minimal background in [warm sand colour], soft diffused studio light from the left, subtle reflection, high-end advertising style, ultra sharp, 1:1 for an online store.
```

**Tips**

- Photograph your product straight on with good light first.
- Ask for a pure white background version for marketplaces like Amazon.
- Make a 4:5 version for Instagram ads.
