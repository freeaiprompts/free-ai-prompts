## New Year’s Eve Glam Party Portrait

Sequins, champagne, confetti and fireworks: a glossy New Year’s Eve portrait made from your own photo.

**Works with:** Nano Banana, ChatGPT Images, Midjourney, Ideogram  
**Type:** Photo

```text
Create a glamorous New Year's Eve party portrait of the person in the uploaded photo, keeping the face 100% identical. [She/He] wears a [sparkly gold sequin dress / black velvet suit], holding a glass of champagne, gold and silver confetti falling, huge "[2027]" balloon numbers and a city skyline with fireworks through a window behind. Flash photography style, glossy, high fashion, vertical 4:5.
```

**Tips**

- Change the year in the brackets to the coming year.
- Ask for "midnight countdown clock" for extra drama.
- Use a waist-up photo for the best outfit result.

## New Year Resolution Planner That Works

Turn vague resolutions into a realistic 12-month plan with milestones, weekly habits and a printable summary.

**Works with:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity  
**Type:** Text

```text
Act as my personal life coach. Help me turn my New Year goals into a realistic 12-month plan. My goals: [goal 1], [goal 2], [goal 3]. I have about [hours] hours per week. For each goal: 1) make it specific and measurable, 2) split it into 4 quarterly milestones, 3) give 3 weekly habits, 4) predict the biggest obstacle and how to beat it. Finish with a one-page summary table I can print.
```

**Tips**

- Be honest about your free time — the plan adapts to it.
- Ask it to "check in with me" monthly by saving the chat.
- Add "make it fun" for gamified habits.

Example results and the interactive prompt builder: [New Year Resolution Planner That Works](https://freeaiprompts.net/prompt/new-year-resolution-planner-that-works)

**Type:** Photo

Frozen in time: a glowing life-size ice sculpture of you at a winter festival, lit in blue and violet under New Year fireworks.

**Type:** Photo


- Great for a New Year's countdown post.
